KETSO tree photo upload

Example: Amara, mango, Makombeh, or tree code
You can take a photo with your phone, or upload an image from your laptop or computer.
Use Take selfie if you want to use the front camera.
Use Take photo if you want to photograph a tree, object, nursery, classroom or activity.
Use Take photo or upload file if you already have a photo saved, or if you are working from a laptop or computer.
1. Take a photo or upload an image
2. Use the zoom buttons to zoom in
3. Drag the image to position it
4. Adjust the crop box
5. Click "Crop to 600 px"
6. Click "Upload original + cropped"
7. Use JPG, PNG or WebP. HEIC is not reliable in browser preview/crop.
8. This cropper page supports image files only. Use the separate Academy upload page for videos, documents or text files.
