KETSO upload

Example: Amara, mango, Makombeh, or tree code
You can take a photo with your phone, or upload an image, video, document or text file from your laptop or computer.
Images will be cropped to 600 px before upload.
Videos, PDF, Word and text files will be uploaded directly without cropping.
For images:
1. Take a photo or upload an image
2. Use the zoom buttons to zoom in
3. Drag the image to position it
4. Adjust the crop box
5. Click "Crop to 600 px"
6. Click "Upload original + cropped"

For video, PDF, Word or text files:
1. Click "Take photo or upload file"
2. Select your file
3. Click "Upload selected file"

Use JPG, PNG or WebP for images. HEIC is not reliable in browser preview/crop.
