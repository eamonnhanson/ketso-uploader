Image/photo upload:
1. Use the zoom buttons to zoom in
2. Drag the image to position it
3. Adjust the crop box
4. Click "Crop to 600 px"
5. Click "Upload work"
6. Use JPG, PNG or WebP. HEIC is not reliable in browser preview/crop.
Document, text and video uploads will be stored for review. Large videos should be kept short.
